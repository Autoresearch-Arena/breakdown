## [0.8.3] - 27-November-2026

### One design for the whole game

Author: Claude Opus 5.5 Medium, coordinating builders at the same level in five waves. Plan:
plans/26-Nov-2026-ui-rethink-plan.md, packages 2 to 12; the rules: plans/26-Nov-2026-design-language.md.

- What: Every screen now follows one design: one calm dark panel, one style of number with its unit, one shape of button, the same card for every mode, the table always the biggest thing on screen, a thumb dock on the phone, marks only on the folding phone's table half, a new Ride camera, achievements with Try it, one share picture, and one name for each shot.
- **The rules and the kit (packages 2 and 3).** One text scale by role, one figure family (lead, control, inline) with
  grey units never under the caption size, one precision rule per unit, spacing steps, three corner radii. A
  component kit in App/Kit (primary, secondary and quiet buttons, panel, mode, result and ruling cards, difficulty
  dots, figure tiles, chips, progress chips, list rows, section labels, the sheet page, the scoreboard, the shot reel,
  the swing bar). The old font names are gone, and scripts/design-lint.py finds no bare colour, font size or corner
  radius left in the app.
- **The shot console (4).** One console on every big screen: "Your strike" with what the ball gets, then the controls
  with Shoot and Try again; planned against delivered only in the Last shot card; the reel only after a shot. Beside
  a big screen's column the reel and the Physics Lens, Ride and Aim tabs sit under the table, and the controls keep
  their whole height so Shoot always shows. The first minute is the table, one sentence, the tip, the swing and Break.
- **The mode frame (5).** Every mode fills in the same mode, result and ruling cards; five difficulty dots
  everywhere; a scoreboard on every match; the lists use one row with a small table drawing; the cloth card spells
  out its figures; How forgiving tiles where a mode sets up a shot.
- **The front door and the sheets (7).** On big screens the table fills the stage and the menu is a side column;
  every sheet uses the sheet page; Options in four groups; Tables as full cards with real figures.
- **The Physics Lens notebook (9).** One current beat drives the chip, the row and the reel's tick; repeats fold into
  one row; the full key opens from the legend; the jump side view gives height and clearance in cm.
- **The phone (6).** Three zones: the title row, the whole table, the thumb dock with a fixed word strip and More, the
  reel and the action row; every action in the dock.
- **The open folding phone (8).** The table half draws marks only; every label moves to the panel's top line.
- **Achievements and sharing (10), shot names (11), Ride (12).** A summary band, the real threshold on each shot
  achievement and Try it; one share picture drawn from the real table; stop, follow, draw, throw, massé and jump named
  one way everywhere; a low chase camera for Ride that says what it is showing.
- 161 new phrases translated into the eleven other languages.
- Not done here: the store pictures are not re-shot for the new design yet.

## [0.8.1] - 26-November-2026

### Shot of the day, the strike panel, a front door that shows the table

Author: Claude Opus 5.5 Medium, coordinating eight builders at the same level in two waves. Plans: start at
plans/25-Nov-2026-award-scores-order-plan.md; how it was built: plans/26-Nov-2026-v0710-coordination.md.
Built as 0.7.10; the Boss named the version 0.8.1 for the size of the change.

- What: A new Shot of the day, the same for everyone, with five tries, a score, a streak, a share card and Game Center boards; before every shot the strike in real units, and after it planned beside delivered; a menu that no longer hides the table; shorter captions that never move faster than you can read; honest store words; and controls for either hand, without colour or timed gestures.
- **Shot of the day.** One shot a day for every player, chosen by the date in Coordinated Universal Time, from a bank
  of 105 shots in a weekly rhythm (Monday an easy pot with position, Tuesday a bank or kick, Wednesday a combination
  or carom, Thursday spin, Friday bumper pool, five-pin or bar billiards, Saturday a strange surface, Sunday a jump or
  massé). Five tries; the score is points for the try that made it plus points for stopping the white ball near the
  mark. The answer stays hidden until the day is finished. The streak, a share picture that gives nothing away, three
  new achievements. It is the first row of the menu. From the second launch on, an unplayed shot of the day waits on
  the table behind the menu; the first launch is unchanged. Trick shots and daily shots now start on their own table.
  BreakProbe has a new "daily" command that authored the bank; "verify" plays every one of them.
- **Game Center.** A daily board that resets at midnight UTC, a longest-streak board and every achievement, set up on
  App Store Connect by scripts/gamecenter-setup.py. The game never asks to sign in at launch: only the first press of
  Board does. No corner badge over the table. "Hide the board" in Options turns it all off. The privacy page says the
  game itself still sends nothing.
- **The strike panel.** While aiming on iPad, Mac and the open folding phone, the notebook half shows the strike in
  numbers: swing, the speed the ball leaves at, push, force (marked "about"), energy, spin, tip, cue angle, cut and
  bridge. Tap a tile to type an exact value. After the shot each tile shows planned and delivered. The tip picker,
  power bar and a new cue-angle dial are large on big screens; one hint, under the table. The upright phone opens the
  same panel from its swing figure. A mode that sets its own cue angle locks the dial.
- **Fewer words.** Every caption has a short form (heading, one figure, one sentence) with the full text behind a tap;
  the lit caption never changes faster than it can be read; a break is one row; Reduce Motion stops the list
  scrolling by itself. Rough edges fixed: "Foul" said twice, the cloth card drawn twice, zero tiles before a shot, a
  caption cut off with an ellipsis, "Player 1" in store pictures.
- **Honest claims.** The store page in all twelve languages, the README and the store notes claim only what a reviewer
  can check: "Nothing is faked" and the "real" boasts are gone. A stylus line and a line for the classroom added.
  Not uploaded until the Boss has read the English.
- **Front door and table view.** One line per menu row in three groups (Today, Ways to play, More), the table in view
  behind it, the game's own accent colour; the Tables page shows the four tables as pictures. On an upright iPad the
  table's half ends where the table does. The floor round the table is lit instead of black. When the Lens zooms in,
  a small plan of the table shows where the camera is looking.
- **Everyone can play.** Sliding paths are dashed for everyone, rolling solid; the scrub bar's marks differ by shape;
  zoom has a button and a menu as well as gestures; "Controls on the left" in Options mirrors the controls and swaps
  the halves; the daily mark, result and strike tiles are spoken; the daily result card works from the keyboard and a
  controller. A page for teachers (store/pages/teachers.md), linked from How to play.
- Fixed at the merge: a daily shot's surface is never saved as the player's own.
- **No wasted space while aiming (looked at on a Mac window and an upright 13-inch iPad).** The Boss found the big
  screen's notebook split in two while aiming: a small controls card under an empty half, the strike's tiles squeezed
  into the other. While "Your shot" is open the notebook is now one column at every width; the twelve tiles sit in
  even rows (six, four, three or two a row) and grow to fill the room, with larger figures when tall. Where the
  controls card has no room for one row, it is the tip picture, the cue-angle dial and Shoot on one row and the power
  bar with the small buttons under it, so nothing floats alone.
- **The aiming panel, redesigned (Claude Opus 5.5 Medium; plans/26-Nov-2026-aiming-panel-redesign-plan.md).**
  What: while aiming on iPad, Mac and the open folding phone the panel reads in three bands. What the ball gets (the
  cut, the speed it leaves at, top or back spin, side spin) in four big white figures; the physics (push, force,
  energy) on one quiet line; and what you set, where each control carries its own figure, printed once: a flat
  technical tip drawing with its two millimetre figures, the swing over a power bar with a metres-per-second scale and
  its calm-limit mark, a cue-angle dial with degrees, the bridge with minus and plus, and Shoot as the biggest control
  in the corner under your hand ("Rolling" in an outline while the balls move). Cyan only on what you can move; a
  dotted underline means "tap to type it"; after a shot a warm yellow marks only what came out different from the
  plan, drawn on the controls themselves and worded ("+4.8 vs 11.9 planned", "as planned"). The plain sentence sits

## [0.7.9] - 25-November-2026

### The jump replay, whole

Author: Claude Opus 5.5 Medium (with three builders at the same level for the clip, the tests and the store preview
route). Plan: plans/24-Nov-2026-jump-replay-plan.md, "Next".

- What: The jump replay comes in closer and tells the whole story. A side picture shows the cue coming down on the ball, the push into the table and the bounce back up, then every hop until the ball settles, and it shows in shared clips too.
- **Closer on the jump, for real.** The Boss found the zoom weak. Measured on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator
  (-frameDebug), the jump over a blocker held its take-off at 1.3 times. Two causes: the take-off's rectangle held
  every hop within a second, and the ball "jumped" was the wrong one. CaptionEngine.flightTop counted the 11, which
  the cue ball flies into at the end of its flight, as passed over with a rounding error to spare, so the card said
  it "passes over the 11 with 0.0 cm to spare", the side view drew the 11 as the blocker and the frame ran all the way
  to it. flightTop now leaves out any ball the flight touches; the card names the 6 (1.3 cm to spare). The take-off
  frames its first flight only, cut a ball's width past the ball it clears (PacePlan.takeOff). The landing card had
  the same kind of slip: after the mid-air hit on the 11 it said the cue ball "comes down 1 cm from where it left the
  cloth"; the distance now runs from where the ball last left the cloth, across a mid-air touch (about 80 cm).
- **Nothing covers the jump on the upright phone.** The explanation blurb, anchored under the cue ball, now also keeps
  clear of the jump's framed stretch and moves once if the camera's glide brings it underneath (PhoneExplanation), and
  a jump's moment sits a little higher there (MomentFrame.flightShareScale) so the blurb fits below it. On that screen
  the side view sits inside the blurb, under its figures (Blurb's new picture slot, FlightSideView's inline style);
  there is no room for a separate panel as well. Other layouts keep the floating glass panel.
- **Why it jumps.** When a raised cue strike made the jump, the side view now starts at the strike: the cue comes down
  onto the ball at its real tilt (CaptionEngine.raisedCueAngle, read back from the take-off exactly as the strike's
  caption reads it), with an arrow down into the slate and an arrow back up off the cloth, both on one scale for the
  whole jump, so the slate's smaller give-back shows as a shorter arrow. A faint band under the cloth stands for the
  slate.
- **Through the landing.** The side view is the jump's whole story (FlightShape.story): every hop from the first
  take-off until the ball stays on the cloth, split where it comes down. It stays up from the strike's caption through
  the take-off's and every landing's (one panel per jump, FlightStoryKey), so it no longer vanishes halfway. A
  landing's caption brightens the distance that flight carried along the cloth and the arrows of that landing's push.
  The height marker is always the first flight's, the number the take-off card's title says.
- **In shared clips.** Lens clips draw the same side view on a dark card (glass cannot go into a video), from the
  strike's or take-off's hold until the ball has finished flying, fading in and out, placed clear of the note and
  never left out (ClipFlight, ClipOverlay). A clip of a trick shot or a lesson now holds the same moments as the
  game's own replay of it (ClipOffer.source takes model.paceSignature when there is no highlight): without them the
  App Store's short jump clip had let the take-off go. The self-test that films the App Store previews works the
  trick's moments out from its -trickPlay name, since the trick only sets them once its answer has played. The App
  Store's jump preview is filmed again with all of it, in all twelve languages.
- The panel is a little taller (124 points, or 184 by 92 when crowded) to fit the arrows under the cloth. No new words.
- Tests: BreakdownTests/JumpReplayTests.swift (the story and its parts) and BreakdownTests/ClipFlightTests.swift (which
  clip frames carry the side view, and where it goes).

## [0.7.7] - 23-November-2026

### Five new features

Author: Claude Opus 5.5 High (coordinator), with five planners, five critics and five builders at Claude Opus 5.5 High. Plans: plans/23-Nov-2026-v077-coordination.md and the five feature plans beside it.

- What: Five new things to try. Share any good shot as a short clip with the Lens explaining it. Big hits now freeze for a moment with the camera swinging round the balls. Replay your shot on every surface at once. An Achievements list in the menu. And Make it again, which saves a lucky free-play shot as your own challenge.
- Why: The Boss picked these five on 23 November 2026; clips to share is the top priority.
- How: each feature planned, checked against the code by a second reader, built by its own developer in its own worktree, then merged, compiled once and tested.

### Fixes after the first clips

Author: Claude Opus 5.5 High.

- What: Clips now show the table as bright as the game does; the room round the table has no wood left (a dark carpet and painted wall panels), so the table's rails stand out; a jump in the Lens replay is framed on its whole flight and the ball it jumps, instead of one ball on an empty grid.
- Why: The Boss found the room's wooden walls fighting the table's rails, and the jump replay hard to read.
- How: the clip's rendered picture is read as linear light (it was being darkened twice); SurfaceTextures.roomFloorImage and TableRoom's wainscot and waist rail; PacePlan.focusRegion frames take-offs and landings with a new flight(of:around:in:).

### Lens clips to share

What: After a shot worth watching, one tap makes a short film of it, ready to save or send through the device's own share sheet. The film shows the shot at full speed on the warm table with its real contact sounds, then the melt into the Physics Lens, a silent rewind, and the explained replay. The replay stops on up to four moments, each with a ring on the ball and one plain sentence in the game's language. A quiet end card closes it with the shot's name and the game's name. A film runs about 25 seconds and never more than 40. It is upright by default on iPhone and wide on iPad and Mac, and the other shape is one more choice in the menu. While the clip is made, a small note shows a progress bar and Cancel. Then it says "Your clip is ready" with Share and Close. Starts: the upright phone strip's "Share clip" after a remarkable shot has been watched or its card closed; the More menu; a round share button beside the Surface button on a sideways phone, iPad, Mac and the open Duo; the highlight card; "Share your best shot" on the cleared table; and the Mac's Playback menu (Shift-Command-E). Nothing is offered in the first minute and nothing pops up by itself.
Why: The Boss called sharing Lens clips a huge priority. A shot explained by the Lens is the game's own hook, and a short film of it is the easiest thing for a player to pass on.
How: The film is made frame by frame from the recorded shot on a second, offscreen copy of the table. It uses RealityKit's offscreen renderer, Core Image and the system video writer, and the game keeps playing meanwhile. A new scene clock lets that copy's camera and Lens eases run on the film's own time, so the same shot always makes the same film. The sound uses the game's own contact voices, mixed offline exactly as play would sound them, with nothing while a ball rolls. The file quietly carries the shot's settings for a later "open a shared shot". New code is in App/Clips with tests in BreakdownTests, and shared files carry small hooks marked "0.7.7 clips:".

### Bullet time on the hit

What: Bullet time on the hit. Once in a while, on a hit worth stopping for, the game stops the clock. This happens on a jump that lands on its ball, the end of a curving shot, a kiss that sends a ball into a pocket, a near-full-power break, or a thin cut that drops. Playback slows as the ball arrives, the click and a firm buzz come on the touch, and the two balls turn a little glassy. The camera drops to the cloth and swings round them while the line of the push, the tangent and the split of their speeds hang in the air between them. A short line of words names the moment. Then time lets go and the camera rises back to the table. Why: the moments people remember deserved a beat of their own in ordinary play, without opening the Lens. How: a pure judge reads the shot once at the strike, and a ration keeps the common kinds rare. It never happens during the opening, a lesson, the drill, a timed game, the what-if, the Lens, Ride or a replay. Scrubbing, Replay, a new shot or leaving the table ends it at once, and the camera eases back instead of cutting. A switch in Options, "Freeze the big hits", turns it off. With Reduce Motion the camera stays still. No new sound was added: only the contact's own click plays. On the upright phone the words are the title row's small status line, beside the table they sit in the header label, and on the open Duo they go to the notebook half. Author: Claude Opus 5.5 High.

### Achievements

What: A new Achievements entry in the main menu lists, in five groups (Shots, Games, Lessons, Trick shots, Behind the 8-Ball), the interesting things the player has done. Shot achievements cover a dead stop, a draw, a massé curve, a jump over a ball, a thrown pot, a combination, a kick, a bank, a carom, two at once, a ball in off the jaw, a ball rattled out, a ball too fast to drop, a big break, running the rack, off a bumper and the red pin alone. The rest are beating the computer at each level, a shot on every table and every surface, each lesson and every lesson, each trick shot and every trick shot, and a medal on every Behind the 8-Ball spot. Every one is always listed. An earned one shows its date, one plain sentence about the physics and a small drawing of the player's own shot; its page has "Watch it in the Lens", which takes the player to that table and surface and replays their shot slowly, holding the moments that made it special. Afterwards a small note says "That was your Bank shot", with a button back to the list. When one is earned, a small note shows only after the balls stop. It makes no sound, has nothing to dismiss, fades by itself and never covers the middle of the table. Nothing shows during the first-launch opening, and the first menu carries no "new" sign.

Why: The Boss liked the physics-journal idea and asked for it as an achievements list.

How: The new folder App/Achievements/ holds the catalogue, the words, the kept shot as a recipe that is solved again through solverCopy, a pure judge that reuses ShotHighlightJudge and adds the jaw, rattle, carom, jump-over and big-break checks, the save (one UserDefaults key, lenient decoder), the live book the model owns, and the views. Small hooks marked "0.7.7 achievements" sit in ShowpieceModel (strike, finishStrike, advance, the book accessor, watchAchievementShot, three readers), ShowpieceView, NotebookPanel (a new achievements page) and TitleScreen (the menu row and its mark). Everything stays on the device; Game Center can be added later without changing anything the player has earned. Strokes the game plays for the player, and test launches, never earn. New words with all eleven translations are in localization/pending/v077-achievements.json. Three new test files cover the judge, the save and the book.

### Same shot, every surface

Same shot, every surface (Claude Opus 5.5 High). What: after your own shot has come to rest, "Every surface" (phone More menu, the Surface menu, the Mac's Playback > Every Surface... with Shift-Command-U) plays the exact same stroke on all ten playing surfaces at once and fans the struck ball's path for each over the table in its own colour, each ending in a tag with that surface's symbol, with one plain sentence on the most striking difference ("On Ice the cue ball travels 6.1 m; here it went 2.0 m.") and the surface's own explanation behind a tap. Pick a surface to watch its balls roll as ghosts beside yours; Watch them roll replays with every ghost; in free play, Play on switches the game to that surface and plays exactly the line that was drawn. Works on all four tables and after a judged try at a trick shot (where it also says when another surface would have made it), a lesson or a Behind the 8-Ball situation, where it is for looking only. Silent, never opens by itself, never on a break, in a match or in the first minute. Why: the Boss asked for the same shot on every world (23-Nov-2026). How: one task group solves the nine other surfaces in parallel from the attempt's own input, table and physics generation (ShowpieceModel.solverCopy), each landing as soon as it is solved; two new package readings, BallTravel (how far a ball went and how much of it skidding) and WorldContrast (the first difference that holds, in a fixed order, and which surface the fan's sentence is about), pick the words from the recordings; the phone shows a chip panel in the bottom bar and an anchored blurb, other layouts a card (the notebook on iPad, Mac and the open Duo, so the table half shows only lines and tags); the controller's d-pad picks, A plays on, B closes once the balls stop.

### Make it again

Make it again (0.7.7) - Claude Opus 5.5 High.
What: after a shot in free play that drops a ball, without the cue ball going down, a new button, Make it again, keeps the table as it stood before the shot, what the shot did and the exact stroke that did it. That becomes the player's own challenge on a new shelf, "Your shots", at the end of the Trick shots list. The button is on the worth-watching card and the upright phone's blurb, in Shot options after Undo last shot, in the Mac Playback menu (Shift-Command-S) and on a controller's left stick click. Once saved it reads Play it now. A saved shot plays like any trick shot. The goal is in words ("Pot the 3 into the foot-left corner pocket. Touch the 3 first.") and the pockets are ringed on the cloth. Watch it replays your own stroke exactly, then slowly in the Lens. A miss gets the judge's usual advice. Each row shows the line that worked, can be renamed or deleted inside the row (no pop-up covers the table on the open Duo), and shows whether it has been made again. Saving makes no sound, and the first minute is unchanged.
