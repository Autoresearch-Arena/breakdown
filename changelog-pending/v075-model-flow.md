Author: Claude Opus 5.5 High (stream model-flow)

- What: The first minute loses its pulsing "Break cue picked" card: the hand-over shows a racked pool table and one instruction, and the break cue is still picked. Practice, the intro-end card's Practice and Watch the intro always rack or play on the pool table, even after another table was played, and the intro-end card's Practice opens Your shot. Trick shots, Learn and Behind the 8-Ball open one list at a time, and every route into play (Practice, Watch the intro, Continue match, a match, the stroke drill, a trick shot, a lesson or a situation starting) closes any list left open. Continue match is offered only for a saved game that can really be loaded; a Continue that fails keeps the menu up and the row goes. The menu names a saved game with the match words' own sentence, the one the Tables pages use. The computer's level is remembered across launches. The Tables list ticks the table actually on screen, and none while a trick shot, lesson, situation, the drill or a match is up. Watch the intro's end card now also appears after the intro was paused or scrubbed, and Menu then Continue during the intro keeps it playing with its sound. Continue on a paused held moment plays on. The speed menu's Auto is ready for the views (it brings back the paced Lens replay). A guided Lens replay turns Ride off only for as long as it plays, and Ride comes back when it ends or is left; Watch it in the Lens on a plain match break is guided too. Behind the menu, a stroke, Play or Pause, a speed and Try again do nothing. Opening the menu during or after a Lens replay turns the Lens off, so the Lens is not left drawn behind the menu and the table is in the ordinary view on Continue. The eight-ball rules add one sentence: from behind the head line, a ball also behind the line can't be hit first unless the cue ball crosses the line. How to play and the Tables list no longer overflow the Mac's notebook panel. Out-of-date header text in the menu and the opening's views now says what a first launch really does.
- Why: The verified bug review of 0.7.4 and the coordinator's play of 0.7.4 on the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator: the first minute should be calm; Practice after bumper pool racked bumper pool; two lists could stack; a broken save offered a Continue that did nothing; the level was forgotten on relaunch; the Tables tick could name a table that was not on screen; the intro's card and Continue on a held moment did not always work; Ride stayed off after a guided replay; keys acted behind the menu; and the Lens stayed drawn behind the menu.
- How: In ShowpieceModel, startGame and watchBreak put free play back on the pool table (stored as the choice) before they rack or solve, and end any trick shot or situation that is up; showList, closeAllLists, tableOnScreen, setAutoRate and holdRideForGuidedReplay are filled in; the Continue row is seeded from a save that decodes, and continueSavedMatch's result decides whether the menu closes; computerLevel is stored under a new GameSettings key; advance notes the intro's end whenever the balls are at rest and gives held Ride back at the end of a guided replay; closeMenu plays the intro on; nextBeat plays on after ending a hold; fireStroke, togglePlay, setRate and both forms of tryAgain return at once while the menu is open, recording no refusal; openMenu ends a guided Lens replay (not a lesson's or trick shot's own demonstration, not the intro). EquipmentCard draws nothing during the hand-over. The free-play cards step aside while a what-if is showing and shrink to their heading during a guided Lens replay on an upright phone. The new rules sentence is its own string, so the paragraph before it keeps its translations; it is translated in all eleven languages.
- Checked: code read against the real APIs; BreakdownTests/ModelFlowTests.swift covers Practice and Watch the intro after another table, the lists, the remembered level, tableOnScreen, Continue on a paused held moment, Auto, the menu backstops, the menu ending a guided replay, and Ride coming back. Not compiled or run here: the coordinator compiles once after the merge and runs the tests.
