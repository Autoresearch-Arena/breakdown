Author: Claude Opus 5.5 High (trick-shots stream)

- What: The trick-shot list is a gallery: each row has a small drawing of the answer's real solved path (the cue ball magenta while it slides, cyan while it rolls, white dashed in the air; the other balls thin white), so the massé's curve and the jump's hop show before anything is tapped. The answer's slow Lens replay holds the moments the shot is about. After a make, "Watch yours in the Lens" replays the player's own stroke the same way. Once the answer has been watched, "Show the answer's path" lays the answer's cue-ball path faintly on the cloth beside the player's own try (white, long dashes, labelled "Answer"). Two new shots: Squirt (medium: pot the 2 with strong left side, which pushes the cue ball off the cue's line, so the aim has to allow for it) and Side Off the Rail (hard: a one-cushion kick round two blockers with strong left side, which widens the angle off the rail). Two-Rail Kick's idea no longer promises side spin it does not use; Draw-Massé Carom has its "Show the maths" line.
- Why: The Boss called the trick shots the coolest part of the game. The list now shows what each shot does, the player can see their own make the way the answer was shown and compare a miss with the answer by eye, and two side-spin effects the captions already explain finally have a shot that stages them.
- How: TrickPathThumb solves each answer once per run, off the main actor, when the list first appears (never at launch), exactly as BreakProbe verify plays it, and samples it into stretches by kind of motion; TrickShotList draws it beside each row (above the text at accessibility sizes; hidden from VoiceOver; a failed solve shows the row as before). TrickShotMode sets the pace signature (TrickSignature, from the lens-pacing stream) before the answer's replay and before Watch yours, keeps the answer's cue path when the answer is watched, and resets Show the answer's path on begin, next, end and watch. GhostOverlay's path drawing moved into a shared TablePathStroke (the ghost looks exactly as before), which the trick-shot layer uses for the answer's path. The two shots were found with new BreakProbe searches ("tricks squirt side-off-rail"), and each is kept only if it teaches what its words say: for Squirt, the same stroke aimed dead straight misses and the answer's aim without side misses (strong side turns the cue ball 2.27 degrees off the cue's line); for Side Off the Rail, the same aim and power without side fails, a no-side stroke launched on exactly the answer's line also fails, and the side widens the angle off the rail (70.4 against 67.7 degrees from the rail's normal). Both are judged with a strong-side rule, at least 9 mm of side on the answer's side of centre (one number, AuthoredTricks.strongSide), because the cue's line is free and a plain stroke aimed where the side would send the ball makes the goal without the spin the shot is about; a try that drops the ball without it is told "In, but without the strong side this shot is about." The windows, as half-widths either side of the answer: Squirt aim 0.70 degrees, power 3.45 m/s, tip height 7.0 mm, side 1.5 mm inside the rule; Side Off the Rail aim 0.54 degrees, power 0.70 m/s, tip height 9.0 mm, side 2.0 mm inside the rule. The plan's "aim window at least 0.4 degrees and power window at least 0.2 m/s" was read as those half-widths.
- Checked: BreakProbe verify passes 45 of 45 authored shots on generation two, the two new ones among them; the package's AuthoredLibraryTests pass (7 tests, including a new one that keeps each side-spin shot's side window inside the strong-side rule); the gallery's sampling was run on every answer outside the app (every cue path starts at its cue ball with no gaps; the jump's has its stretch in the air). Not compiled or run in the app by this stream (the coordinator builds once after the merge). The massé answer's sliding curve measures 7.6 degrees (9.5 with its landing), so TrickPathThumbTests checks for 5 degrees, not the plan's 10.
