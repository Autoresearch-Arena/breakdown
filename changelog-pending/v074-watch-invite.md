### Worth watching (stream watch-invite, Claude Opus 5.5 High)

- What: After a remarkable shot in free play (a jump, a curve, a thrown pot, a combination, a kick, a bank, two at once, a big draw, a dead stop, a ball too fast for the pocket, a bumper into the cup, the red pin alone, and the break from the second rack on), one slim card in the card slot names the physics in a line, with "Watch it in the Lens" and, when a trick shot teaches that kind and it is not made yet, "Try the trick shot". It goes the moment the player touches aim, power, spin or the cue's angle, or closes it; nothing plays by itself; no sound, no praise. In a match, a remarkable shot by either seat (rare kinds every time, common ones once a game) shows the ruling card with the shot named and "Watch it in the Lens". Table cleared now sounds the bigger made-it once and offers "Watch your best shot", which replays the rack's best remarkable shot in the Lens and then puts the cleared table back exactly. Watch the intro ends on a small card offering Trick shots and Practice instead of a frozen frame.
- Why: The Boss wants players guided, at natural moments and never pushily, to watch the coolest shots and see the physics. A player's own lucky shot is the best doorway to the Lens and to the trick shot of the same kind, and the two dead ends (the end of the intro, a cleared table) now lead somewhere.
- How: New App/HighlightOffer.swift (the pure frequency rule: rare kinds every time, a common kind once per rack and at least three shots after the last common card, the trick link once per kind per session and never for a made trick shot) and App/HighlightWords.swift (a title and a line per kind, generic physics words only). ShowpieceModel's new "Worth watching" section judges each shot once with ShotHighlightJudge, last in finishStrike so nothing it already did changes, and only once the opening is over (the first break of a first launch is never judged), outside the trick shots, situations, lessons and the drill; the result is stored with the recording it belongs to, so the card and the Mac menu read stored state. A retry's comparison card is never joined by a highlight card. The rack's best shot is kept as the attempt exactly as it was solved; its replay writes back what that shot was solved from before loading it, and afterwards writes back the clearing shot's own (its recording at its end, the controls, the retry ghost, the Lens, the speed, Ride) without recording or counting anything; Menu, Rack again, Undo, a surface change, a stroke, a break and Try again put the cleared table back first (any control the player set during the replay is kept), and anything else that loads a table simply ends the replay. The break's card waits for the session's second free-play break, so the first break of a launch stays as calm as 0.7.3. MatchMode keeps the offered highlight per ruling (lensHighlight) and the common kinds offered per game (not saved, cleared by rematch); a ruling that offers one becomes the card. The model's offerLensReplayOfBreak and watchBreakInLens became offerLensReplay and watchShotInLens. "Watch it in the Lens" and "Watch your best shot" turn Ride off first whenever they hold a highlight's beats, since guided pacing and Ride do not mix (the best shot's replay puts Ride back afterwards). HighlightCard and IntroEndCard live in PlayCards, so they sit in the card slot (and the notebook on the open Duo), never over the table. 27 new strings are translated in all eleven languages (localization/pending/v074-watch-invite.json).
- Checked: written without compiling, as the release plan asks (one compile after the merge); BreakdownTests/HighlightOfferTests.swift covers the frequency rule, the match rule and the words. Until the lens-pacing stream fills ShotHighlightJudge, no shot is judged remarkable, so none of the new cards appear and the match card only offers the break's replay, exactly as before.
