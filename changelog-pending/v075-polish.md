Stream: polish. Builder: Claude Opus 5.5 High.

- What: The "worth watching" card stops naming ordinary shots: a draw only counts when the shot also sent something down (a pocket, a cup, a hole, or a pin that scores) or the cue ball came back well over a metre, and a shot the computer played is offered only when it was rare (a jump, a curve, a throw, a combination, a kick), a break, or it scored. Five-pin's menu icon is a small cluster of dots instead of a bowling figure. On the five-pin table the pins are a brighter white and a clear red, and a faint castle mark (a thin ring just outside the pins and a soft disc under each one) shows where the target is from the playing camera on a phone. The trick-shot gallery's drawings are small tables: a dim cloth-green bed with a rail edge, six dark pocket dots, the balls in their own colours and slightly thicker paths.
- Why: The Boss played 0.7.4 on the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator: a five-pin shot by the computer that scored nothing was offered as "Drawn back"; five-pin's icon was a person bowling; the pins and the castle were nearly invisible on a phone; the gallery thumbnails read as thin empty outlines.
- How: ShotHighlightJudge gains sentBallDown (an object ball in any pocket or hole, the struck ball in a bed hole, or a five-pin pin knocked by a ball other than the struck one) and plainDrawFactor; the draw rule now needs sentBallDown or twice drawDistance, so it only narrows (nothing that offered no card before can offer one now). HighlightOffer.offersComputerShot is the computer rule as one pure function; its call belongs in MatchMode.rule (match-ui's file), noted for the coordinator. TableKind's five-pin symbol is circle.hexagongrid (SF Symbols 3; the five-dot cross is the menu's Play button). FivePinTable: new pin finishes (these dress both the stand-in pins and the turned Blender pins), the code-built stand-in pins drawn at the pin's true 10 mm width, and one translucent flat mesh (MeshBuilder) for the castle ring and spot discs that lightens whatever surface is down; no pin, radius, height, position or parameter changed, so every five-pin shot solves exactly as before. Once the turned pins load they replace the stand-ins, so there only the new colours and the cloth mark show. TrickPathThumb's drawing only: the bed, rail, pocket dots, ball colours (BallTextures.swatch, the 3D balls' own palette) and line widths; TrickPath's data, the row size and the hidden-from-VoiceOver drawing are unchanged. No new user-facing words, so no translation fragment.
- Checked: written without an app build (the coordinator compiles once after the merge). New BreakdownTests/HighlightNoiseTests: a pool draw and a five-pin draw by the computer that scored nothing are no longer drawn back and offer nothing (each first shown to be a draw the 0.7.4 rule offered), a draw that pots still is, and the computer rule for every kind. The test shots' numbers were measured on the package solver (cloth, seed 1): the pool plain draw comes back 0.91 m with nothing down, the five-pin one 0.94 m with no pin down, the potting draw 1.13 m with the 1 down; every authored trick answer was re-solved and the Long Bridge Draw still comes back 1.43 m with two balls down, so ShotHighlightJudgeTests' expectations stand unchanged. The symbol was checked against the system's symbol list and the app's other menu symbols.
