Author: Claude Opus 5.5 High (learn-modes stream)

- What: The trick shots, the lessons and Behind the 8-Ball read the cue angle properly. Your turn after Watch it keeps the raised cue you had set. A trick shot or lesson struck at the wrong cue angle is told "Raise the cue more. A level cue cannot do this one." or "The cue is too steep. Lower it a little." instead of "So close" or a line about the aim. "Too full" and "too thin" are only said for a direct shot, never for a kick or a raised cue. The draw, follow and side advice says how far to move the tip ("Not enough draw. Strike about 10.5 millimetres lower on the cue ball."), and the fine controls show the tip and the side in millimetres beside the word ("follow, +8.5 mm"). The tip picture snaps each axis to its centre line on its own, so a plain draw carries no stray side. The Massé, Jump the Blocker and the Draw-Massé Carom are struck on angles the cue-angle menu offers (20, 30 and 15 degrees), and the carom on a tip-height window you can set. In Squirt and Side Off the Rail a tap on a ball points the cue itself at it, so allowing for the squirt is yours to do, as the shot teaches. Behind the 8-Ball judges the angle the shot was struck with and shows one cue-angle control (the shot panel's), not two, and its Lens replay puts Ride back afterwards. Stop Dead draws its finish circle. Changing the cloth while a trick's answer or a lesson's demonstration plays no longer stalls the explanation. The three cards step aside while a what-if is open and shrink to their heading and main button during a guided Lens replay on a phone held upright. On the Mac the three lists fit the notebook. The pull-back's ticks follow the Vibration option, and the spoken bar-billiards ruling says a fallen white peg loses "this break's points".
- Why: The shots a player can only make with a raised cue gave advice that could never work at a level cue, and their answers sat on angles nobody could set, so Watch it and Your turn disagreed. The millimetres turn "strike lower" into a number the controls show. The rest are the verified gaps in the learn modes from the 0.7.4 review.
- How: TrickShotJudge has one cue-angle check (cueAngleLine), run before the ranking of aim, power and tip whenever the answer raises the cue: a trick uses the run of offered angles its answer makes it on (AuthoredTricks.cueAngleBands, carried by TrickShot.cueAngles), a lesson passes its own marked band, and LessonMode.judge now relies on it (the two sentences are the lesson's old ones, word for word). tooThick returns nil when the answer has an elevation or the attempt's own recording has the cue ball meeting a cushion before its first ball. TrickShotMode keeps the attempt's elevation (and the control's, before the answer plays) in the player's settings; EightBallMode hands its judge attempt.elevation and calls model.holdRideForGuidedReplay. The re-authoring is a new BreakProbe search ("tricks masse-offered jump-offered draw-masse-carom", TrickAuthoring.swift): the old strike first, moved onto each offered angle within 12 degrees and re-centred in its windows; only when none keeps the shot's checks, a grid near it on those angles. Every result is kept only if a level cue still misses (for the carom also no draw and no side), and its tip-height window is at least 1.5 mm each side. The Massé: the old stroke at 20 degrees (it also makes it at 25 only with its own aim; 20 was nearer the hard band), aim 51.68, power 1.55 m/s, tip centre, side +13.5 mm; makes it only at 20. The Jump: the old stroke at 30 degrees, aim 26.94, power 5.05 m/s, tip -1.0 mm; makes it at 30, 40, 50 (and 70). The Carom: no offered angle kept the old stroke, so a grid of 184,080 strokes; at 15 degrees, aim 353.63, power 3.10 m/s, tip -6.0 mm (window -7.5 to -4.0 mm), side +9.0 mm; makes it at 5, 10 and 15. Each elevation is AuthoredLessons.sine(forAngle:) exactly, and the windows and runs are in AuthoredTricks' comments with the log paths (Team 4TB drive, breakdown-build/v075-learn-modes-*.log). BreakProbe verify now also reports every raised-cue trick and lesson at every offered angle beside the band its judge uses, without changing the exit status. Surface changes: both modes fill recordingReplaced (the shared part is RecordingFollow in TrickShotMode.swift): the versions they keep follow the re-solved recording; a trick answer being explained is rebuilt from the new recording (its path, its moments) and replayed in the Lens; a lesson's moment is found again and a replaying or paused demonstration replays to it. Stop Dead's circle and every goal ring are drawn by one GoalMarks (TrickShotViews.swift) for both layers, on the ghost overlay's ring path. The per-axis snap is 0.09 of the dot's travel (about 1.5 mm), off on an axis a lesson marks. In Squirt and Side Off the Rail (TrickGoal.side set) AimOverlay.aimLaunch points model.aim(toward:) at the spot and skips AimGeometry.cueAim(forLaunch:), and the spoken answer adds "The cue points at the 2 ball; the side will push the ball off it." The Mac lists' minimum frames are nil when the inlinePages environment value is true.
- Checked: BreakProbe verify passes 45 of 45 authored shots on generation two with the new answers, and its report shows each raised-cue answer making it on every angle of its recorded run (Massé 20; Jump 30 to 50; Carom 5 to 15; the curve and jump lessons on their own bands). The package tests pass (222 tests), including two new AuthoredLibraryTests: every raised-cue trick's elevation is exactly the sine of an offered angle inside its run, and every trick's tip-height window is at least 1.5 mm each side. New app tests (not run by this stream; the coordinator compiles and runs them once after the merge): TrickShotJudgeTests (a level cue at the Massé, the Jump and the Carom gets the raise line; the Massé at 80 degrees and the Carom at 30 get the lower line; every raised answer sits on an offered angle inside its run; a Two-Rail Kick aim miss is never "too full" or "too thin"; the long draw struck at centre height is told the millimetres; Your turn after Watch it keeps a raised angle) and LessonTests (the lesson angle lines come out of the shared check). The new answers change what other streams' existing tests solve: ShotHighlightJudgeTests (the Massé read as a curve, the Jump as a jump), SignatureBeatTests and TrickPathThumbTests (the Massé's sliding bend of 5 degrees or more) were not run here; BreakProbe's report shows the new Massé's cue ball turning a long way before its first ball, so the curve is there, but those three test classes are the ones to watch in the coordinator's run.
