### Matches speak to you (stream match-ui, Claude Opus 5.5 High)

- What: In a game against the computer, and in bar billiards alone, the match now talks to the person as "you": "Your shot", "You have the solids.", "You shoot again.", "Ball in hand for you.", "You win!", and score and player rows read "You". A name the player types is kept and used instead. The names editor only offers your own seat ("Your name") and can never rename the computer. The shot that ends a game is headed "You win" with a trophy, or the computer's win with a flag, and says why (an eight-ball loss card is no longer empty, three fouls in a row says the game was lost); the win sound plays only when you win. When the computer answers a choice it says what it chose ("Computer (Hard) takes the table as it lies."). The result card adds your record against this level ("Against Hard: won 3 of 7.") and, from the second game in a row, "This sitting: you 2, computer 1." Nine-ball's banner says who is on two fouls. Ball in hand behind the head line explains that a ball also behind the line can't be hit first, and the new head-line foul has words. The saved game's line says "Your shot." or "The computer's shot.", and a saved game that could never be continued is deleted. A match break's "Watch it in the Lens" now waits for a tap instead of vanishing after a second and a half. On the other tables: the banner never says "to break", describes the computer's turn instead of giving you orders, and fouls name the balls by colour ("the red was hit before the yellow", "the struck ball touched no ball"), never "the cue ball" or a number. Bar billiards alone no longer shows "This break: 0", no longer repeats "Time is up." after every shot, says the white peg costs "this break's" points, says the game also ends when nothing is left on the table to hit, and its Rematch says it racks a game against the clock. While a guided Lens replay plays on an upright phone, the ruling card shrinks to its heading and its button.
- Why: The verified match bugs of 0.7.4: a match called the one person "Player 1", ended a won game under "Keep shooting", cheered the computer's win, chose silently for the computer, and on the other tables talked about cue balls and the 9 and gave the person orders during the computer's turn.
- How: MatchWords gains personSeat and you (the seat that is not the computer, or the only seat alone, while its name is still the default, in this language or English for old saves, or empty) and a second-person form of every sentence that names a seat; nothing ever stores "You". The game-over reason in a ruling now gives the end sentence, and when the game was won by pocketing the 8 or the 9 the pot line leaves that ball out (it lists only what else dropped), so the winning ball is said once. A choice answer gives one sentence naming the chooser in place of the re-rack, spotted-ball and "shoots again" lines; ball in hand and a turn passing are still said after it (nothing after a re-rack, which already says who breaks). MatchWords.foul takes a FoulContext (the game, the shooter, whether that is you, and five-pin's target ball, MatchMode.targetBall) so the table games get their own words. MatchMode: moment(for:winnerIsComputer:) reads the winner from the after-state at both call sites; the computer's answer becomes the settled banner line; an in-memory sitting tally (sittingWins, sittingGames) bumped where each finished game is recorded, kept by rematch, never saved; the settle beat does not run while a break's ruling offers the Lens; setPlayerName ignores the computer's seat; SavedMatchSummary gains computerToShoot; loadSaved deletes a save that does not decode, has another format, is over, or seats two people with no computer (a file that cannot be read from disk is left alone). MatchViews: the ruling heading branch for a result, trophy or flag from the person's seat; the result card drops the reason the ruling card already gave and adds the level record (OpponentWords.levelRecord) and the sitting line (OpponentWords.sitting), also in the spoken result; the End-this-game confirmation uses MatchMode.confirmingEnd (from the foundation) so the menu back button can stand aside; the aiming card's Lens offer, which the model never offered there, is removed; lensReplayCompact slims the ruling. TableGameWords: the computer's-turn banner lines, the person's foul-points lines, "You" in spoken scores, and the reworded time-up line and rules step. 57 new strings are translated in all eleven languages (localization/pending/v075-match-ui.json).
- Checked: written without compiling, as the release plan asks (one compile after the merge).
