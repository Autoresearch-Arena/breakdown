### Layouts and input (stream layouts, Claude Opus 5.5 High)

- What: Nothing acts on the table from behind the menu any more: the title row, the phone's controls and the side column go quiet (no Return or Escape from hidden buttons), the back button gives up Escape while the menu is open or End-this-game is asking, and on the Mac the Shot and Playback menus and the toolbar's play, replay, speed and Your shot are greyed. With the side column (a phone turned sideways, a small window) the menu covers the whole window and the column goes blank behind it without the table moving. On the Mac, Learn, Trick Shots and Behind the 8-Ball open at once and close each other and any page; Continue Match, New Free Game, the drill and Watch the Intro close any page, and a Continue Match whose save will not load leaves the menu up; Game > Tables re-racks on every pick and ticks only the table actually on screen; Next Explained Moment moves a guided Lens replay on; Speed has Auto; Playback has What If... (Shift-Command-T); the toolbar's Your shot is plain and greyed on the computer's turn. A controller's buttons leave the table alone while the menu or a list is up; its Menu button opens the menu or goes back to the game; A continues a held Lens moment; the options button is Try again and a right stick click Replay with the Lens (Watch it in the Lens when a shot is on offer). The notebook (iPad, Mac, the open Duo) gets Continue for a held moment above its controls, the Lens colour key in its own row under the top bar (with Explanations off too), shows only its one line during the opening instead of the friction constants, never draws onto the table, and with Explanations off in two columns moves the cards into the empty half. The open Duo's table and the table beside the notebook are framed without a phantom title row, so they are a little larger, and the caption chip stays on the table half. With the notebook put away on a wide window the controls take less height and the title row reaches the corners. On a phone held upright a guided Lens replay slims the mode cards and Your shot waits closed until the balls stop, and in a match Your shot also waits closed while a ruling, hand-over or result card with its own button is up. iPad's "Play here" now says the game in the other window ends.
- Why: The verified gap review found keys and controller buttons working the table behind the menu, menus opening lists behind other lists, a Tables menu that could not re-rack the table already ticked and ticked the wrong one, no way to continue a held moment on the big screens or the controller, the Lens key vanishing with Explanations off, a blank notebook half, the first minute's notebook showing physics constants beside the one instruction, a table framed under a title row that is not there, a caption chip crossing onto the words on the Duo, and, on the Boss's phone, the table squeezed to its top third by a match card stacked over an open Your shot.
- How: ShowpieceView disables the title row and bottom stack behind the menu, makes MenuBackButton's Escape optional, draws TitleScreen in the outer ZStack for the side-column layout, hides and disables the side column while the menu is up, reads the window's own shape for the bottom stack's height caps (0.45 or 0.62 on a wide window, 0.74 or 0.62 on a phone) and caps only the controls at 760 points, sets lensReplayCompact on the bottom stack, keeps one remembered-panel flag for both the guided replay and the match card (ruling card, hand-over, result), closes an open what-if when Your shot opens, and reports the stage's frame to LeaderState.stageRect, which CaptionLeader's chip placement now bounds and clamps to. StageChrome gains hasHeader, so SceneBridge.request() and the Lens callouts fall back to the bare safe area where there is no title row. NotebookPanel clips to its frame, shows the now internal ContinueButton at the top of its lower edge while the model holds a beat, puts LensLegendChip in a measured row under the top bar (subtracted from the one-column budget with its gap) and moves the cards to the second column when Explanations is off; NotebookStory leaves out the surface card while the opening runs, the panel's numbers strip (which carries the same figures) waits for the opening to end too, and the story no longer carries the key. MacChrome wraps the Shot and Playback menus in a disabled group behind the menu, routes the lists through model.showList, makes Tables a menu of toggles that always call playTable with the tick from model.tableOnScreen, adds Auto (model.setAutoRate) and What If... (model.whatIf.open, enabled by canOpen), and Next Explained Moment calls nextBeat while guided. ControllerInput has one page-up check for every button but Menu and for the sticks, a toggling Menu button, A's nextBeat before the play and pause fallbacks, and the optional options and right stick buttons; How to play's Controls page reads the updated mapping. Nine new strings are translated in all eleven languages (localization/pending/v075-layouts.json).
