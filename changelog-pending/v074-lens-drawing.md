Author: Claude Opus 5.5 High (lens-drawing stream)

- What: When a guided Lens replay stops on a hit between two balls, the table now shows the geometry of that hit: a dashed line through both balls' centres, the tangent line across the point where they touched (finer dashes, or dots with Differentiate Without Colour on), and three arrows that close into a triangle: the striking ball's speed in (white), and both balls' speeds out (cyan), drawn head to tail. A small square mark appears when the two balls leave at a right angle. On a shot's own key moment (a trick shot's or a highlight's signature beat) the view also eases in a little toward that moment and springs back when the hold ends.
- Why: The plan's top "make the invisible visible" item: the right-angle and half-ball rules, drawn by the game's own solver at every hit the replay explains, and close enough to read the spin on the spotted cue ball.
- How: A new pure helper, LensContactGeometry, reads the hit's recorded event (which ball struck, the line of centres, the speeds before and after). The Lens builds the lines, arrows and mark once and only moves and fades them; they show only while a hit is being held, fade in over a quarter second, and are left out entirely when the struck ball was already moving (then only the two lines) or when the throw drawing already shows that hit's line of centres. The arrows share one scale so the triangle still closes. The close-up asks the hand zoom to come in 1.8 times about the contact (or the ball, or the cushion, pocket or pin the moment is about), only from the normal view, never during Ride or with Reduce Motion on, and gives the view back only if the player has not pinched or tapped in between. New zoom calls closeUp and atFittedView carry it.
- Checked: written without a build (builders do not compile; the coordinator compiles once after the merge). New tests, LensContactGeometryTests: the triangle closes on the first hit of every trick-shot answer played on the cloth; a stop shot is not marked square; exact hand-made hits check the square mark, the striker found either way round, a struck ball already moving, events that are not a two-ball hit, the close-up's point and the zoom's close-up and spring back. Not yet run.
- Note for the Boss: the square mark is honest and therefore rare. The game's balls lose a little bounce and grip each other slightly, as real ones do, so a stun hit at a half-ball cut leaves at about 85 degrees rather than 90; only thinner cuts land inside the two-degree mark. The lines and the triangle show every hit regardless.
