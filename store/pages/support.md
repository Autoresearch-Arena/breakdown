# Breakdown: See the Physics. Support

Breakdown is a pool game whose table shows its own physics. Learn the shots in six short lessons, play eight-ball or
nine-ball against a friend or the computer, try the trick shots and Behind the 8-Ball, or play bumper pool, five-pin
billiards and bar billiards. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and on the Mac with macOS 26 or later.

If something is wrong or confusing, write to mark@markbarney.net. Say which device you play on and what you were
doing when it happened; a screenshot helps.

## How do I take a shot?

- On a touch screen: drag on the table to aim, then pull back from the white ball and push through it, like a real
  stroke. How fast you push is how hard you hit. If you would rather set the power and press a button, turn on
  the Options switch that plays a shot only from the Shoot button.
- With a mouse or trackpad: press on the white ball, pull back and let go.
- With a keyboard or a game controller: aim and set the power with the keys or sticks, then press Return or the
  controller's shoot button.

How to play, in the menu, shows the same for whichever way you play.

## The game says it cannot shoot yet

A card under the table always says why. Usually it is one of these: in eight-ball you have not called a ball and
pocket yet (and there is no obvious one to suggest), it is the computer's turn, a ruling is still waiting for you, or
the last shot is still playing.

## Where are the lessons and the games?

Open the menu. Learn holds the six lessons, in order. Play holds eight-ball and nine-ball, against a friend on the same
device or the computer (easy, medium or hard), and the three other tables. Practice is a free table. Trick shots and
Behind the 8-Ball are in the menu too.

## What is the Physics Lens?

Turn it on after any shot and the replay slows down and explains itself: amber paths where a ball is sliding, cyan
where it rolls, rings where balls hit, and a short sentence for each moment that matters. Turn on "Show the maths" in
Options to see the equation behind every sentence. The tips that explain the colours show the first time; "Show the
Lens tips again" in Options brings them back. "Watch the intro" in the menu plays the opening break again.

## Does my progress move to my other devices?

No. Everything is saved on the device you play on and nowhere else, so each device keeps its own progress. Deleting
the game deletes it.

## Can I play without looking at the screen?

Yes. The table works with VoiceOver: every ball and pocket can be found and aimed at, and each shot is described when it
ends. The game also follows larger text sizes, Reduce Motion and Differentiate Without Colour, and can be played with a
game controller.

## Privacy

The game collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. The privacy policy says so in full.
