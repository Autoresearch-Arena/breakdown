# Breakdown: See the Physics. Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Breakdown is a pool game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. The short version of this page: it collects nothing about you,
it sends nothing anywhere, and it does not track you.

## What stays on your device

There is no account and no sign-in. Your settings (sound, haptics, the aim guide, the surface and table you chose),
the lessons you have passed, the trick shots you have made, your Behind the 8-Ball scores and medals, your record
against the computer and a game of pool you left halfway are all saved on your device, inside the game's own storage,
and nowhere else. They are not copied to any server. If you delete the game, they go with it.

## What the game does not do

The game makes no internet connections. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites, no
in-app purchases, no chat and no way to share anything with other players. It does not ask for your name, your email
address, your contacts, your location, your photos, your camera or your microphone. A two-player game is played by two
people on the same device, so nothing is sent between devices either.

A game controller, if you connect one, is read only to move the cue and press buttons in the game.

## Crash reports and beta testing

If you have chosen to share crash reports and app analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may
pass anonymous crash reports for this game to its developer. If you play a test version through Apple's TestFlight,
Apple handles the installation and passes on any feedback, screenshots or crash reports you choose to send. Apple's
own privacy policy covers what Apple collects through TestFlight and the App Store.

## Children

The game is for everyone. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing about a child to
collect, share or delete.

## Changes to this page

If the game ever changes what it does with information, this page changes first and the date at the top moves.
